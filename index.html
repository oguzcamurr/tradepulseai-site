Signals

Signals you can read.

BUY, SELL and Watchlist signals for a list of major crypto futures pairs, recalculated every hour on 1-hour candles. Each one shows RSI, trend strength, volatility and liquidity, the market regime and timeframe, and the price at the signal next to the current futures price.

The 0–100 score shows how strongly the model's inputs agree. It is not a probability of profit, and a high score does not mean a trade will work. How to read a signal score.

Simulated

AI Positions

See what the models are doing.

AI Positions are opened by TradePulse's models in simulation. No real money is traded. Each one shows its entry, the current price, its return before fees, and its take-profit, stop-loss or time exit. Closed positions keep their result, losses included.

Why this position? · Pro

A plain-language reason.

For each simulated position, Pro shows a simplified description of the kind of market evidence behind its opening, plus a summary of what is happening to it now: price versus entry, levels and time open. It does not reveal the model's exact rules.

My Positions

Track what you hold elsewhere.

Log a position you hold on an exchange: symbol, direction, entry, size, and optionally leverage, take profit and stop loss. TradePulse shows its live profit or loss from public futures prices. Your entries are stored only on your device, and TradePulse never places, copies or manages trades.

  • What the "AI" models are. TradePulse's models are rule-based and statistical: fixed rules and thresholds applied to price and volume data, with thresholds chosen from analysis of historical market data. They do not use machine learning or a language model, and they do not learn from your activity.
  • Signals. Recalculated every hour for each covered pair. A label (BUY, SELL or Watchlist) comes with a 0–100 score that shows how strongly the inputs agree. The score is not a forecast or a probability of profit.
  • AI Positions. Simulated positions opened by the models, shown before fees, with no real money involved. Simulated results are not executed trades and do not predict future results.
  • My Positions. Positions you enter yourself, kept on your device. TradePulse never connects to your exchange account or trades for you.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does TradePulse AI give financial advice?

No. TradePulse AI provides market information and education only. It does not know your finances or goals, it does not give personal recommendations, and every decision is your own.

What does the 0–100 signal score mean?

It shows how strongly the model's inputs (trend, volatility, liquidity, RSI and MACD momentum) line up with the conditions the model looks for. It is not a probability of profit and not a prediction. See how to read a signal score.

Is "AI" in TradePulse machine learning?

No. "AI Positions" and "AI Performance" are feature names for the output of rule-based, statistical models: fixed rules and thresholds applied to price and volume data. They do not use machine learning or a language model.

Are AI Positions real trades?

No. They are simulated positions opened by the models. No real money is traded, returns are shown before fees, and simulated results do not guarantee or predict future results.

Is TradePulse AI free?

The app is free to download and includes a limited set of signals, AI Positions and My Positions. TradePulse Pro is an optional monthly subscription that unlocks full lists, position details, "Why this position?" and AI Performance.

Does TradePulse connect to my exchange or place trades?

No. There is no exchange login. Positions you add to My Positions are stored on your device, and TradePulse never places, copies or manages trades.

Is my data private?

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal data. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is collected.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app is available in English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish and Turkish.
